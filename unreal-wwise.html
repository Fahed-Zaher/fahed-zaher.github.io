Implementation

Interactive Audio Systems

Designing and implementing audio that responds to gameplay, player actions, and the surrounding environment.

My implementation work focuses on integrating sound into the game engine so that audio responds naturally to gameplay, player actions and the surrounding environment.

I work primarily with Wwise and game engines such as Unity and Unreal Engine, combining sound design with interactive audio systems.
