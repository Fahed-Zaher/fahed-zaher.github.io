Case Study

Unreal × Wwise

Interactive Audio & Spatial Audio Showcase

This project explores the implementation of interactive game audio using Wwise and Unreal Engine.

Rather than building a single large gameplay system, the project is structured as a collection of focused environments, with each room designed to explore a different audio implementation technique.

Overview

Five Audio-Focused Environments

A compact Unreal project built around practical Wwise implementation.

Spatial Audio

Acoustic maze and occlusion environments exploring positional and obstructed sound.

Environment

A sci-fi laboratory soundscape built around localized environmental audio.

Dynamic Audio

Surface-based footsteps, landing sounds, and RTPC-driven interactive music.

Engine Unreal Engine 5.8.2
Middleware Wwise 2025.1
Spatial Audio

Acoustic Maze

Exploring positional audio through a maze environment.

The Acoustic Maze was designed to explore how spatial audio behaves as the player moves through a large environment containing multiple paths, corners, and dead ends.

The environment provides a simple way to evaluate how the perceived position and direction of sounds change as the player navigates through the space.

The maze was intentionally kept focused on the audio behavior rather than gameplay, making it easier to hear changes in spatial positioning while moving through the environment.

Occlusion Room

Exploring obstruction between the listener and sound sources.

A separate environment was created to test how sound behaves when the direct path between the player and a sound source becomes obstructed.

The setup allows different positions around the environment to demonstrate the difference between unobstructed and obstructed sound.

Environmental Audio

Sci-Fi Environmental Soundscape

Building a localized environmental atmosphere.

This environment is a small sci-fi laboratory built around computers, equipment, and other interactive-looking elements. Each element provides an opportunity to introduce localized environmental sounds and build a more believable sense of place.

The goal was to create an environment that feels active without relying on gameplay events, using individual 3D sound sources to give different areas of the room their own sonic character.

The result is a compact environmental soundscape that demonstrates how individual spatialized sounds can work together to establish the identity of a location.

Dynamic Footsteps

Surface-Based Footsteps & Landing

Footstep and landing sounds driven by the environment.

The Dynamic Footsteps room focuses on connecting player movement with the environment underneath the character.

Unreal Engine surface detection is used to identify the surface beneath the player, allowing different footstep sounds to be triggered depending on the material being walked across.

Surface Detection

Unreal Engine surface information determines which audio variation should be played.

Footsteps

Footstep events are triggered dynamically from the character's movement.

Landing

Landing audio is integrated alongside the movement system.

Interactive Music

RTPC-Driven Vertical Layering

Dynamically blending musical layers during gameplay.

The Mixing Room explores interactive music through vertical layering. Multiple musical stems are loaded into Wwise and their balance is controlled dynamically rather than switching between completely separate pieces of music.

An RTPC is used to control the relationship between the musical layers, allowing the soundtrack to gradually change as the player's movement changes.

Stems

Individual musical layers are prepared as separate stems.

RTPC

Player movement controls the musical balance.

Layering

Musical elements can fade in and out independently.

Transitions

Changes occur continuously rather than through hard switches.
