Unreal × Wwise

Interactive Game Audio & Spatial Systems

Overview

This project explores interactive game audio implementation using Unreal Engine and Wwise. Rather than building a single traditional game level, I created a series of dedicated audio environments, each designed to demonstrate a specific implementation system.

The project focuses on spatial audio, environmental sound, dynamic footsteps, occlusion and interactive music, with each system implemented and tested directly inside Unreal Engine.

Engine & Middleware

Unreal Engine

Unreal Engine 5.8.2

Used as the primary game engine for level design, Blueprint implementation, gameplay interaction and audio system integration.

Wwise

Wwise 2025.1.10

Used for interactive audio implementation, spatialization, attenuation, obstruction, RTPC-driven systems and music integration.

Systems Explored

Spatial Audio

Explored 3D positioning, attenuation and spatial behavior through dedicated environments designed to demonstrate how sound changes within a space.

Environmental Audio

Built a sci-fi environmental soundscape using layered ambient elements and positional sounds to create a cohesive space.

Dynamic Footsteps

Implemented surface-based footsteps and landing audio using Unreal's surface detection and Blueprint-driven event logic.

Occlusion & Obstruction

Explored how environmental geometry affects 3D sound by implementing obstruction and occlusion behavior between the listener and audio sources.

Interactive Music

Implemented vertical music layering using RTPC-driven control to smoothly introduce and remove musical layers based on player movement.

Spatial Audio

Acoustic Maze & Occlusion Room

The spatial audio section focuses on how positioning, distance and environmental geometry influence the player's perception of sound.

Acoustic Maze

A large maze environment designed to demonstrate spatial audio behavior. Multiple sound sources are placed throughout the environment, allowing the listener to navigate through different spaces while identifying sounds based on their position.

Mini Occlusion Room

A compact environment created to demonstrate how obstacles between the listener and a sound source can alter the perceived sound. The implementation explores obstruction and occlusion behavior as the player moves around the environment.

Environmental Audio

Sci-Fi Environmental Soundscape

This environment focuses on creating a believable sci-fi space through layered environmental sounds, ambient elements and positional audio sources.

Dynamic Footsteps

Surface-Based Footstep & Landing Audio

The footsteps system uses Unreal Engine's surface detection to determine which footstep and landing sounds should be triggered by the player's movement.

The system was implemented through Blueprint logic and animation notify events, allowing the appropriate audio event to be triggered according to the detected surface.

Interactive Music

RTPC-Driven Vertical Layering

The music system demonstrates vertical layering using an RTPC-driven approach. Instead of switching between different musical states, individual layers are faded in and out according to the player's movement.

The musical stems begin together and are dynamically controlled to create a gradual transition between different levels of musical intensity.

Final Result

The project brings together several core interactive audio systems inside Unreal Engine, with each environment focusing on a specific implementation challenge.

The result is a compact showcase of spatial audio, environmental sound, gameplay-driven audio, occlusion and interactive music implemented through Wwise.

What I Learned

This project strengthened my understanding of implementing interactive audio directly within Unreal Engine and gave me practical experience translating sound design concepts into responsive gameplay systems.

It also allowed me to explore different approaches to spatial audio, dynamic gameplay audio and interactive music while working with Wwise in a Blueprint-focused workflow.
